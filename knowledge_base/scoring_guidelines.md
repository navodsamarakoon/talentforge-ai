# TalentForge Scoring Guidelines

TalentForge AI uses a 0 to 10 scoring scale for each evaluation criterion.

## Score 0-2 — Very Poor

The candidate demonstrates little or no understanding of the evaluated
criterion.

Typical characteristics:

- Fundamental misunderstanding
- Incorrect implementation
- Major logical problems
- No meaningful technical reasoning

---

## Score 3-4 — Poor

The candidate demonstrates limited understanding.

Typical characteristics:

- Significant weaknesses
- Frequent mistakes
- Incomplete solution
- Poor technical reasoning
- Important requirements are not addressed

---

## Score 5-6 — Average

The candidate demonstrates basic competency.

Typical characteristics:

- Partially effective solution
- Understands the basic concept
- Some technical weaknesses
- May use an inefficient approach
- Limited consideration of edge cases

---

## Score 7-8 — Good

The candidate demonstrates solid technical competency.

Typical characteristics:

- Correct or mostly correct solution
- Appropriate approach
- Good code quality
- Reasonable technical reasoning
- Minor weaknesses may exist

---

## Score 9-10 — Excellent

The candidate demonstrates strong technical competency.

Typical characteristics:

- Correct and robust solution
- Appropriate algorithm and data structures
- Strong efficiency
- High-quality implementation
- Clear technical explanation
- Good awareness of edge cases and trade-offs

---

## Overall Evaluation Principle

Scores should be based on evidence from the candidate submission.

TalentForge AI should not assign a high score simply because a candidate
claims to understand a concept.

Evidence should come from:

- Submitted code
- Test results
- Candidate explanation
- Demonstrated technical reasoning
